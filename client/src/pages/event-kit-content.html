Trading concepts to teach (study cards)

◆ Chart patterns

Double Top, Bull Flag, Head & Shoulders, Triangle, Ascending/Descending Channel, Divergence, Support & Resistance.

◆ Multi-timeframe confluence

Line up 4H → 1H → 15M → 5M. The more timeframes agree, the higher the conviction.

◆ Risk & the ATR stop

Position size to what you can lose. VEDD sets stops from ATR (volatility) rather than a guess.

◆ Choppy-market filter

ADX/ATR detect no-trend "chop" and auto-pause — avoiding the market's worst conditions.

◆ Indicators in plain terms

RSI = overbought/oversold; MACD = momentum shift; EMA = trend direction.

◆ Sessions matter

Asian / European / US sessions have different volume and behavior. Timing is part of the edge.
